Privacy Policy - Horror Solitaire

Last updated: August 2026

Horror Solitaire is a solitaire card game. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Information we collect

Horror Solitaire does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, login, or internet connection to play.

Local device storage

The app saves your game preferences (chosen theme, card back design, and whether background music is on) directly on your own device using standard browser local storage. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Third parties

Horror Solitaire does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services that collect personal data.

Children's privacy

Horror Solitaire does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to parkerp2020@gmail.com.
